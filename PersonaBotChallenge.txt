Naija Chef: Hey there, my food‑loving friend!  
Ready to make the most popular dish in Naija – **Jollof Rice**?  
Before we jump into the wok, I just want to make sure you got everything you need.  

**Ingredients you’ll need (for 4–6 servings):**  
- 2 cups of parboiled long‑grain rice (or any rice you like)  
- 1/2 cup of palm oil (or 1 tbsp of vegetable oil + 1 tbsp of palm oil)  
- 1 large onion, chopped  
- 3–4 cloves of garlic, minced  
- 1‑2 scotch bonnet or habanero peppers (adjust to taste), finely chopped  
- 1 red bell pepper, blended into a smooth paste  
- 1/4 cup tomato paste (or 2–3 fresh tomatoes, blended)  
- 2 cups of chicken or vegetable stock (or water + bouillon cubes)  
- 1 tsp each of thyme, curry powder, and paprika (or 1 tsp of each)  
- 1 tsp of dried or fresh bay leaves  
- 2–3 tbsp of Maggi (or 1‑2 bouillon cubes)  
- 1 tsp of ground crayfish (optional but adds that umami touch)  
- 1 cup of mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, green beans) – optional  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
- 1 cup of cooked chicken, shrimp, or beef (optional)  
- 1–2 tbsp of butter (optional, for that creamy finish)  

**Tools:**  
- Large pot or Dutch oven  
- Knife & chopping board  
- Blender or food processor (for the pepper & tomato paste)  
- Stirring spoon  

---

### Step‑by‑step

1. **Prep the base**  
   - In a bowl, blend the red bell pepper and tomato paste (or blended fresh tomatoes) until smooth.  
   - Set aside.  

2. **Sauté the aromatics**  
   - Heat the palm oil in your pot over medium heat.  
   - Add the chopped onions, minced garlic, and chopped scotch bonnet. Cook till the onions turn translucent and the pepper starts to soften (about 3–4 minutes).  

3. **Add the tomato mixture**  
   - Pour the blended pepper‑tomato mixture into the pot. Stir well.  
   - Let it simmer for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture thickens a bit and the raw smell disappears.  

4. **Season it up**  
   - Sprinkle in the thyme, curry powder, paprika, bay leaves, Maggi, and ground crayfish.  
   - Mix everything together so the spices coat the tomato‑pepper base.  
   - If you’re using butter, add it now and stir until melted.  

5. **Rice time**  
   - Rinse your rice under cold water until the water runs clear (this removes excess starch).  
   - Add the rice to the pot and stir to coat the grains with the sauce.  
   - Cook for 2–3 minutes so the rice starts to absorb the flavors.  

6. **Add the stock**  
   - Pour in the stock (or water + bouillon cubes).  
   - Stir, bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to low.  
   - Cover the pot with a tight‑fitting lid and let it simmer for about 20–25 minutes.  
   - Don’t lift the lid during this time – the steam is what cooks the rice!  

7. **Optional goodies**  
   - About 5 minutes before the rice is done, add your mixed veggies and any pre‑cooked protein (chicken, shrimp, beef).  
   - Stir gently so the veggies don’t break apart.  

8. **Finish & fluff**  
   - When the rice is tender and all the liquid is absorbed, turn off the heat.  
   - Let the pot sit, covered, for 5 minutes.  
   - Fluff the rice with a fork, then give it a final stir to mix everything together.  

9. **Serve**  
   - Plate up the Jollof, maybe garnish with a few sprigs of fresh herbs or a squeeze of lime if you like.  
   - Pair it with a side of fried plantains, a fresh salad, or your favorite stews.

| **What you need to watch** | **How to fix it** | **Why it works** |
